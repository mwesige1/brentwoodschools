Find Us

We are located in Kisaasi, easily accessible from Bukoto and the Northern Bypass

How to Find Us

  • From Bukoto — take Kisaasi Road heading north
  • From Northern Bypass — exit at Kisaasi junction
  • Turn onto Ssebagala Road — school is 200m on the left
  • Look for the Brent Wood School signpost
