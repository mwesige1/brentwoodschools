Who We Are

A School Built on Quality & Excellence

Brent Wood School is a private Nursery and Primary school located in Kisaasi, Kampala, Uganda. We are dedicated to providing a nurturing and stimulating environment where every child can thrive with no discrimination.

Our focus on academic excellence, creativity and character development ensures a strong foundation for future and lifelong learning success. We believe every child is unique and deserves a personalised, caring learning experience.

Our Mission, Vision & Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Our Mission

To provide a high quality, inclusive and stimulating education that empowers every child to reach their full potential academically, socially and emotionally.

Our Vision

To be the leading nursery and primary school in Uganda, recognised for academic excellence, character formation and the holistic development of every learner.

Our Values

Integrity, excellence, respect, inclusivity and compassion form the foundation of our school community and guide every interaction between staff, pupils and parents.
