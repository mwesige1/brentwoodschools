WHO WE ARE

At Brentwood School, we believe every child is uniquely gifted with the potential to achieve greatness. As a thriving nursery and primary school, we provide a dynamic and nurturing learning environment guided by the Competency-Based Curriculum (National Curriculum), where academic excellence is strengthened through creativity, exploration, practical experiences, and outdoor learning.

We are committed to developing well-rounded learners who are confident, disciplined, innovative, and prepared to excel in an ever-changing world. Our approach extends beyond traditional classroom learning by fostering curiosity, critical thinking, problem-solving skills, and a lifelong passion for discovery.

At Brentwood School, diversity is celebrated and every child is embraced as a valued member of our community. We cultivate an atmosphere of respect, inclusion, and belonging, where learners appreciate different cultures, traditions, and faiths while developing strong values of kindness, integrity, responsibility, and compassion.

Through a rich blend of academic learning, digital discovery, hands-on activities, outdoor experiences, and real-world applications, we empower learners to become independent thinkers, effective communicators, and confident leaders. Our dedicated and passionate educators provide personalised guidance and encouragement, ensuring each child is supported to discover their talents and reach their full potential.

At Brentwood School, we do more than educate — we inspire, nurture, and prepare learners to become responsible, creative, and compassionate global citizens.

Brentwood School — Inspiring excellence, building character, and empowering the leaders of tomorrow.

OUR CORE VALUES

Diversity Values

  • Inclusion — Ensuring every learner feels valued and accepted
  • Diversity — Celebrating different cultures, backgrounds, and perspectives
  • Unity — Building a strong sense of togetherness
  • Belonging — Creating a welcoming school community
  • Tolerance — Respecting differences and opinions
  • Cultural Awareness — Appreciating traditions and heritage
  • Teamwork — Achieving goals together
  • Compassion — Supporting and caring for others
  • Citizenship — Developing responsible members of society

Academic & Learning Values

  • Academic Excellence — Striving for the highest standards in learning and achievement
  • Curiosity — Developing a desire to explore, question, and discover
  • Creativity — Encouraging innovation and original thinking
  • Critical Thinking — Developing the ability to analyse, evaluate, and solve problems
  • Lifelong Learning — Inspiring continuous growth and discovery
  • Innovation — Embracing new ideas and technology
  • Collaboration — Working effectively with others
  • Perseverance — Persisting through challenges and difficulties
  • Achievement — Celebrating progress and success

Character Development Values

  • Integrity — Doing what is right with honesty and strong moral principles
  • Respect — Valuing oneself and others

  • Discipline — Demonstrating self-control and positive behaviour

Faith Values
