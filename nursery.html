Our Nursery

Building Foundations for Life

At Brent Wood School Nursery, we believe that the early years of a child's life are the most important. Our dedicated nursery teachers create a warm, safe and stimulating environment where children aged 2 to 5 can flourish.

We focus on the whole child — language, cognitive growth, fine and gross motor skills, and social emotional development so every child becomes a confident and joyful learner ready for the next stage of education.

What We Offer in Nursery

A rich and balanced early childhood programme

Play-Based Learning

Children learn best through play. Our structured play activities develop creativity, problem solving and social skills.

Literacy & Numeracy

Early reading, writing and number work introduced in a fun, engaging and age-appropriate way.

Arts & Creativity

Painting, drawing, music and crafts help children express themselves and develop fine motor skills.

Physical Education

Daily outdoor play and structured PE sessions build gross motor skills, coordination and healthy habits.

Social Development

Group activities and circle time teach children to share, cooperate and build meaningful friendships.

Healthy Meals

Nutritious, balanced meals and snacks served daily to fuel growing bodies and developing minds.

Nursery Classes

Structured learning levels designed for each stage of early childhood

2 – 3 Yrs

Baby Class

Introduction to school life through play, songs, stories and simple routines in a caring environment.

  • Sensory play activities
  • Basic communication skills
  • Toilet training support
  • Social interaction
3 – 4 Yrs

Middle Class

Building on foundational skills with more structured activities, early literacy and numeracy concepts.

  • Phonics and letter recognition
  • Number concepts 1–20
  • Arts and craft projects
  • Outdoor exploration
4 – 5 Yrs

Top Class

Preparing children for Primary One through focused reading, writing, maths and critical thinking activities.

  • Reading and writing readiness
  • Number work up to 100
  • Science exploration
  • Primary One transition prep
