BEGIN YOUR CHILD'S JOURNEY

Choosing a school is one of the most important decisions a family makes. At Brentwood School, we welcome families who want more for their children than results alone — an education that builds character, confidence and curiosity alongside academic excellence.

Our admissions team is here to guide you through every step, from your first enquiry to your child's first day. We warmly invite you to visit our campus, meet our teachers, and see our learning environment for yourself.

Four simple steps

HOW TO APPLY

01

Submit an Application

Complete the form below, or collect a physical form from our school office.

02

Visit & Assessment

Tour the campus with your child and attend a short, friendly placement assessment.

03

Receive an Offer

Successful applicants receive an offer letter with class placement and joining details.

04

Confirm & Enrol

Settle requirements, collect the uniform list, and welcome to Brentwood.

WHAT TO BRING

  • Completed application form
  • Child's birth certificate (original and copy)
  • Four recent passport photographs
  • Immunisation or health card
  • Parent or guardian national ID copy
  • Previous school report card, if applicable
  • Transfer letter, for transferring learners
Bring originals for verification. Copies are retained by the school.
