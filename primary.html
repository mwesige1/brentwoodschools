OUR PRIMARY SECTION

At Brentwood School, we believe that every child possesses unique gifts and limitless potential. Guided by our motto, "Quality and Excellence," our Primary Section provides an exceptional learning environment where academic achievement, character formation, and holistic development come together to prepare learners for a successful future.

Rooted in Uganda's Competency-Based Curriculum (National Curriculum), our programme is designed to develop knowledgeable, skilled, and confident learners who are equipped to thrive in a rapidly changing world. We cultivate curiosity, creativity, critical thinking, collaboration, communication, and digital competence, empowering children to become independent learners and innovative problem-solvers.

Our dedicated and highly qualified educators provide personalised learning experiences that challenge, inspire, and support every learner. Alongside academic excellence, we intentionally nurture faith, discipline, integrity, respect, responsibility, and compassion, helping children develop into principled individuals who make meaningful contributions to their communities.

Confident Achievers Primary One – Seven

At Brentwood School, we are committed to raising confident achievers, compassionate leaders, and lifelong learners — empowering every child to excel today and lead with purpose tomorrow.
