Excellence in Learning Primary One – Seven
Our Primary

Preparing Pupils for the Future

Our Primary section at Brent Wood School provides a structured, supportive and academically rigorous environment for pupils from Primary One to Primary Seven. We are committed to helping every child reach their full potential.

From foundational skills to critical thinking, our experienced teachers steadily prepare pupils for national examinations and future learning through a well-balanced curriculum that covers academics, sports, arts and ICT.

Subjects We Offer

A comprehensive curriculum covering all key areas of learning

Mathematics

Building strong numerical foundations through problem solving, mental maths and applied mathematics.

English Language

Developing reading, writing, comprehension and communication skills for confident expression.

Science

Exploring the natural world through experiments, observations and inquiry-based learning activities.

Social Studies

Understanding Uganda, Africa and the world — history, geography, civics and cultural studies.

ICT & Computing

Hands-on computer literacy, typing, internet safety and basic programming concepts for digital readiness.

Local Languages

Luganda and other local languages taught to promote cultural identity and mother tongue literacy.

Creative Arts

Music, art, drama and crafts nurture creativity, self-expression and cultural appreciation.

Physical Education

Team sports, athletics and physical fitness activities promoting health, teamwork and discipline.

Religious Education

Christian and Islamic Religious Education developing moral values, character and spiritual growth.

Primary Classes

Seven years of structured and progressive academic development

P1

Primary One

Transition from nursery with focus on reading, writing and number work in a nurturing environment.

P2

Primary Two

Building on P1 foundations with more structured literacy, numeracy and science activities.

P3

Primary Three

Expanding subject range with introduction to Social Studies, Local Languages and Creative Arts.

P4

Primary Four

Mid-primary consolidation with more complex concepts across all core subjects and ICT.

P5

Primary Five

Deepening academic rigour with increased focus on critical thinking and problem solving skills.

P6

Primary Six

Intensive preparation begins with mock assessments, revision and exam technique development.

P7

Primary Seven

Final year with full focus on PLE preparation, past papers, mock exams and individual coaching.
